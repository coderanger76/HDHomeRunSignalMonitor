How to use this

  1. Monitor tab → tick the channel(s) that have the problem (for example CBS 38.1 and 42.2).
  2. Press Record. Watch TV normally, on your TV or Tablo, as you always do.
  3. Whenever the picture breaks up, press Picture is pixelated (or froze / sound dropped). Do it right when it happens.
  4. Press Stop when you've seen a few problems, or let it run for a few hours.
  5. Open the Analyze tab, pick the recording. The top card says in plain English what the data points to.

What the numbers mean

  • Signal strength: how much radio power the antenna delivers. Low = antenna, cable, or distance problem. Above about 60% is comfortable.
  • Signal quality / symbol quality: how cleanly the tuner can decode that signal. Should sit at 100%. Drops while strength is fine usually mean interference or reflections (multipath).
  • Data rate (Mbps): the video actually received by this computer from the HDHomeRun.
  • Lost packets / stalls: video data that went missing or paused on the way from the HDHomeRun to this computer. This is a network symptom, not an antenna one.

Good to know

  • Each recorded channel occupies one of the HDHomeRun's tuners. If the Tablo or another TV needs every tuner, a channel there could fail while you record — keep recordings to the channels you need.
  • This page measures the path antenna → HDHomeRun → this computer. If the pixelation is on a different device (Tablo, TV) and everything here looks perfect, the problem is downstream of that point.
  • Your recordings are saved on this computer in the data/sessions folder inside the monitor folder. Nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Keep the Terminal window running. Closing it stops the monitor. The computer is kept awake while recording.
